About FitTrack AI

FitTrack AI is a full-stack fitness and wellness management system built as a final-year college project. It combines a Flask backend, a MySQL database, and a clean vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend to give users a single place to track their weight, workouts, nutrition, steps, and personal fitness goals — with simple, rule-based insights to help keep them motivated.
